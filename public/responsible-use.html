Responsible Use

Permission Matters

A public link can be visible without giving every visitor the right to download, repost, sell, or reuse the media. Use DownloadDash for content you own, content you licensed, content you have permission to save, or content you are otherwise legally allowed to keep.

Creator Rights

Creators can retain copyright and usage rights even when content is visible publicly. Keep attribution and source context with important saved files.

Private and Restricted Content

Do not use DownloadDash to bypass privacy settings, paywalls, account restrictions, deleted posts, or access controls.

When in Doubt

Ask the creator, check the source platform rules, or do not download the file.
