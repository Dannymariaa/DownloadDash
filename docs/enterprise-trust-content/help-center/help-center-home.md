## Quick Answers

### Does every link work?
No. Availability depends on public access and source-platform rules.

### Can private content be processed?
No. DownloadDash supports publicly accessible links only.

### Why are some quality options missing?
Quality depends on the formats made publicly available by the source platform.

### Why did a link stop working?
The content may have expired, been removed, become private, or changed accessibility.
