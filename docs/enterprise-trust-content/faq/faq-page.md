## Does every link work?
No. Availability depends on public access and source-platform rules.

## Can DownloadDash process private content?
No. Private or restricted content is not supported.

## Why is 4K missing?
The source platform may not expose 4K publicly for that media.

## Why did my download fail?
The media may be private, deleted, expired, region-restricted, or unsupported.

## Is DownloadDash only for YouTube?
No. DownloadDash is positioned as a public-media saving platform.

## Does DownloadDash bypass restrictions?
No. It should not bypass login walls, private accounts, or paywalls.
