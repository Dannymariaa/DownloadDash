# Facebook Public Media Guide

DownloadDash may help users process publicly accessible Facebook media links when supported formats are available.

## What May Work
- Public links
- Supported formats
- Available quality options

## What May Not Work
- Private content
- Expired stories
- Login-protected media
- Restricted pages
- Region-locked content

## Reminder
Only save or reuse Facebook content when you have permission or a legal right.
