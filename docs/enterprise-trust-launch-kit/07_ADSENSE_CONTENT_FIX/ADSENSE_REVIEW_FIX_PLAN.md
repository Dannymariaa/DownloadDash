# AdSense Review Fix Plan for DownloadDash

AdSense may reject downloader sites because they look like thin tools, low-value pages, or screens without enough publisher content.

To improve your chance:

## Before Requesting Review

- Add helpful written content on the homepage.
- Add About, Privacy, Terms, Contact, Responsible Use, DMCA, FAQ, Safety, and Guides.
- Remove empty pages.
- Remove under-construction messages.
- Avoid fake buttons.
- Avoid aggressive popups.
- Avoid interstitial ads during review.
- Make sure Google can crawl pages.
- Make sure mobile design is clean.
- Keep ads disabled or minimal until approval.

## Homepage Must Explain

- What DownloadDash does
- What public-link-only means
- Why some links fail
- How users should respect creators
- Why DownloadDash does not ask for passwords
- How to contact support
- Where to read legal policies

## Important

A downloader form alone is not enough. The website must look like a real product with helpful content.
