# Why Some Video Links Fail

Not every media link can be processed successfully.

Common reasons include:

- the post is private,
- the content was deleted,
- the link is incorrect,
- the platform changed its system,
- the content is region-restricted,
- the media format is unsupported,
- the platform blocks automated access.

DownloadDash aims to provide a cleaner experience instead of misleading users with fake promises.
