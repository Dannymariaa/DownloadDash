# How to Respect Creators Online

Creators spend time, energy, and money making content. Saving content does not mean users can automatically repost, sell, or claim it as their own.

## Good Practices

- Credit the original creator.
- Ask permission before reposting.
- Do not remove watermarks or signatures.
- Follow platform rules.
- Avoid using content commercially without rights.

DownloadDash encourages responsible content use.
