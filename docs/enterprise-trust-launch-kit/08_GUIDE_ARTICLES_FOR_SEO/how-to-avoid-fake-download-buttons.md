# How to Avoid Fake Download Buttons

Fake download buttons are common on unsafe websites. They may lead to ads, unwanted software, suspicious pages, or browser notification traps.

## Warning Signs

- Many buttons saying “Download Now”
- Popups that force you to click
- Browser notification requests
- Unknown extensions
- Redirects to unrelated websites
- Password requests
