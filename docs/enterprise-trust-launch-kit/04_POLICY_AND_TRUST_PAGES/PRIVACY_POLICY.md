Effective Date: May 7, 2026

DownloadDash values privacy and transparency. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected and how it may be used.

- browser and device information,
- analytics information,
- error logs,
- abuse-prevention logs,
- messages sent through support or contact forms,

DownloadDash does not ask users for social media passwords.

Information may be used to:

- provide the requested service,

DownloadDash is designed for public links only. Users should not submit private, restricted, or unauthorized links.
