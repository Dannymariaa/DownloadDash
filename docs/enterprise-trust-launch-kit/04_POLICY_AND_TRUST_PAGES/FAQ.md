# DownloadDash FAQ

## What is DownloadDash?

DownloadDash is a public-link media saver for videos and images.

## Does DownloadDash ask for passwords?

No. DownloadDash does not ask for social media passwords.

## Can DownloadDash download private content?

No. DownloadDash is intended for public links only.

## Why does a link fail?

A link may fail because the post is private, deleted, unsupported, region-restricted, blocked, or technically unavailable.

## Is DownloadDash affiliated with social media platforms?

No. DownloadDash is independent and not officially affiliated with third-party social platforms.

## Can I repost downloaded content?

Only if you have permission or a legal right to do so.

## How do I contact DownloadDash?

Use support@downloaddash.store for support and legal@downloaddash.store for legal issues.
