# DMCA and Copyright Policy

DownloadDash respects intellectual property rights.

If you believe your copyrighted work is being misused through DownloadDash, contact:

legal@downloaddash.store

## Include in Your Notice

- your name or organization,
- your contact email,
- the copyrighted work being reported,
- the URL or details of the allegedly infringing content,
- a statement that you believe the use is unauthorized,
- a statement that the information in your notice is accurate,
- your electronic or physical signature.

## Response

DownloadDash may review notices, restrict functionality, remove references where applicable, or take reasonable action.

## Hosting Note

DownloadDash may not host third-party platform content directly. In many cases, copyright owners may also need to contact the original platform where the content is hosted.
