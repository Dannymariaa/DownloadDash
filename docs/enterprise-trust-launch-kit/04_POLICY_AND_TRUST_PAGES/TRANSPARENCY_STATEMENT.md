# DownloadDash Transparency Statement

DownloadDash is an independent public-link media saver focused on cleaner design, clear rules, and responsible use.

## What We Believe

Users deserve tools that are honest and easy to understand. Creators deserve respect. Online platforms have rules. DownloadDash aims to balance useful features with responsible boundaries.

## What We Avoid

- misleading platform affiliation,
- fake download buttons,
- malware,
- suspicious redirects,
- password collection,
- private-content bypassing,
- unclear policies.
