# Responsible Use Policy

DownloadDash is intended for responsible public-link media saving.

## Allowed Use

You may use DownloadDash for:

- saving your own content,
- saving content you have permission to use,
- personal offline viewing where allowed,
- educational or fair-use purposes where legally permitted,
- public content that does not violate creator rights.

## Not Allowed

You may not use DownloadDash for:

- piracy,
- stealing creator content,
- downloading private content,
- bypassing login restrictions,
- reposting without permission,
- harassment,
- impersonation,
- illegal distribution,
- removing creator credit.

## Creator Respect

Creators deserve credit, permission, and fair treatment. DownloadDash encourages users to respect creators and avoid harmful redistribution.
