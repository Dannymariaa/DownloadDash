# DownloadDash Safety Center

DownloadDash is designed to avoid common unsafe downloader-site patterns.

## Safety Commitments

- No fake download buttons
- No hidden malware downloads
- No social media password requests
- No forced browser notification traps
- No intentional private-content bypassing
- Clear policies
- Clear contact emails
- Public-link-only usage

## User Safety Tips

- Use only the official website
- Never enter social media passwords into downloader websites
- Avoid websites with many fake buttons
- Avoid suspicious popups
- Respect copyright and creators
- Keep your browser updated
