## A Cleaner Public Media Saver

DownloadDash helps users process publicly available media links in a cleaner and more transparent way. It is designed for people who want a simple tool without fake download buttons, confusing redirects, or unnecessary password requests.

## Public Links Only

DownloadDash is intended for public links only. Users should not use it to access private accounts, restricted posts, or content they do not have permission to view or use.

## No Social Media Passwords

DownloadDash does not ask for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, or other social media passwords. Users should avoid any downloader website that asks for private login details.

## Why Some Links May Fail

Some links may not work because the original content is private, deleted, region-restricted, unsupported, or blocked by the source platform. DownloadDash does not guarantee that every link from every platform will work.

## Respect Creators

Saving content does not automatically give someone the right to repost, sell, or claim ownership of another creator’s work. Users should respect copyright, credit creators, and follow platform rules.

## Transparent Policies

DownloadDash provides clear Privacy, Terms, DMCA, Responsible Use, Safety, FAQ, and Contact pages so users can understand how the website works.
